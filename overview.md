# Student Study Planner — Feature Overview

- **Syllabus Upload:** Upload course syllabi to keep course information in one place.
- **Deadline Extraction:** Identify assignment due dates and exam dates from uploaded syllabi.
- **Personalized Schedule:** Automatically organize deadlines and study activities into a student schedule.
- **Deadline Reminders:** Notify students about upcoming assignments and exams.
- **Study Session Suggestions:** Recommend study times based on assignment difficulty and available free time.
- **Assignment Dashboard:** View upcoming tasks and deadlines from different courses in one place.
- **Priority Planning:** Highlight urgent or challenging assignments to help students decide what to work on first.
- **Progress Tracking:** Mark assignments as completed and monitor remaining work.
